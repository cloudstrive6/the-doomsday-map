   curve since 2026-11-13; `[:10]` of videos sorted by views is all Shorts; C2 and the whole "first 75 s" fix are unjudged for
   eleven videos. Cost: about one more API call per long video. Without it every other recommendation below is blind on the long side.
2. Spend the next long video on the Hindcast-proven demand: NATO and the alliance's answer to a Russian nuke on Ukraine (not a remake
   of 005). Evidence: 1.92M views, 2,945 vph recent, against our 20 views on the same event. Title must name Ukraine and NATO, use
   "What If", and avoid their "We Mapped Every Strike That Follows".
3. Convert Short viewers to the long video. 15,401 Shorts views produced 118 channel-page views and 85 related-video views; 255 long
   views in the week. Each Short already links the long video in line 1 (CLAUDE.md), so the lever is the pinned comment (needs the
   comment scope, craft.md engine item 4) and the last-3-s pill. Measure: YT_CHANNEL plus RELATED_VIDEO views per week (203 now).
4. Keep clock-time and "What If" named-place titles on Shorts and test the hypothesis: 013 to 015's Shorts, one geometry-first title
   against one clock-first title for the same strike. Evidence: Washington "Minute 17" 1,281 against "100 Kiloton ... Capitol" 533.
   n is 12 Shorts, so it needs about 10 more before acting.
5. Hold the 30% word rule for chapters 01 and 02. 014 is at 37% against the 30% rule; check its curve first. Evidence: the leak on
   001 and 002 was exactly the pre-simulation material (45 to 82 s on both).
6. Run a 48-hour thumbnail swap on one long video (say 008, 7-day read 2026-11-24) and record variant, swap time and CTR before and
   after. Without it, variants cannot be compared. Impressions are too few for small differences; only swap if CTR is under 4%.
7. Lean the long-video titles toward queries people type (YT_SEARCH 520 views is our only scaling long-form source): place + weapon
   + "what if". Evidence: 004 (place plus street-level promise) 5.2% and 002 (place plus first-use) 5.7% on the two samples over 300
   impressions, 001 (generic) 2.3% on 1,502.
8. Do not chase subscribers by changing content for C6. 17 subscribers at 0.7 per 1,000 Shorts views; the lever is volume and the
   Short-to-long handoff (3), not a new format.
