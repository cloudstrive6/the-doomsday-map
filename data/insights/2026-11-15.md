## 2. Retention

Curves exist for 001 and 002 only (003 to 006 have no curve, see caveats). Scores are audience watch ratio at 5% bins;
at 12 minutes a bin is about 37 s. Skill vocabulary (yt-retention): a leak is a slow slide, a cliff a single-bin fall, a
slide a gradual decline after a cliff.

### 001 Russia launches a nuclear strike on America (748.5 s, 49 views, average viewed 26.7%, hold at 11% = 46%)

Curve: 0.98 at 1% (7 s), 0.66 at 6% (45 s), 0.46 at 11% (82 s), 0.39 at 16% (120 s), 0.34 at 21%, 0.25 at 26%, flat
0.23 to 0.27 to 76%, then 0.23, 0.14, 0.09 at the end.

- Cliff 1, 7 s to 45 s (minus 32 points, the fastest fall per second): shots are city 0 to 5.6 s, globe 5.6 to 22 s, then
  an 11 s `text` card (22 to 33 s) carrying the cold open, then globe. Script: the cold open runs "Twenty to four in the
  morning, Nebraska" and the silos opening, then the spoken "this is a simulation, it has not happened". Why it likely
  lost people: the first strike picture is not the payoff, a static text card runs 11 s, and the disclaimer lands about
  35 s in. This is the pattern that moved the opening rules in CLAUDE.md.
- Cliff 2, 45 s to 82 s (minus 20): chapter card 01 (42.7 to 46.7 s), then a 19 s `isotype` card "nuclear warheads in the
  world" (46.7 to 65.7 s), then a 25 s region shot (65.7 to 90.7 s). Script: "About twelve thousand in total... Russia's
  land-based force is spread across eleven missile divisions". It is a warhead inventory at 0:47, which craft rule 3 now bans
  before 1:30. A 19 s card and a 25 s shot also break the 4 s and 8 s rules.
- Slide, 82 s to 195 s (minus 21 more): chapters 01 and 02 stay data (arsenal, bases, the thirty-minute timeline).
  Retention then plateaus at about 25% through the simulation, so the people who survive the first 3 minutes stay.
- Tail, 81% to 96% (0.23 to 0.09): chapters 07 and 08 (history, "the clock"). The viewers who stayed for the simulation
  left when the episode turned to history. This is the finding behind "end on the aftermath".

### 002 China blockades Taiwan (674.6 s, 48 views, average viewed 40.2%, hold at 11% = 51% on the 2026-11-12 curve)

Curve: 0.95 at 1% (7 s), 0.85 at 6% (40 s), 0.51 at 11% (74 s), 0.49, 0.41 at 21%, 0.36 at 31%, flat 0.36 to 76%, 0.31,
0.26, 0.28, 0.28 at the end. Relative retention performance is 0.28 to 0.39 at 11 to 26%, so the 11% to 26% stretch is
weaker than similar-length videos, then 0.73 to 0.81 at 36 to 41% (the point where the simulation starts).

- First 30 seconds worked: 85% still there at 40 s (001 had 66% at 45 s). 002 opens on a region shot (0 to 8 s) of a tanker
  and the gas line, "Taiwan has about eleven days of gas... on day twenty-three a nuclear weapon is used", a number card at
  27 s, a DAY 23 stamp at 31 s. It states the payoff in the first minute.
- Cliff, 40 s to 74 s (minus 34 points, the biggest single fall in either video): shots are the 14 s `text` card
  (37 to 51 s, the spoken "this is a simulation, it has not happened"), a globe (51 to 59 s), the chapter card
  "An island that runs on ships" (59 to 62 s), a number card (62 to 66 s) and a region shot (66 to 85 s). Script: the
  disclaimer paragraph, then "Taiwan imports about ninety-seven percent of its energy". Why: after a hook with a nuclear
  payoff, the viewer gets 14 s of static words, a chapter card and an energy statistics lesson. Same pattern as 001.
- Plateau, 31% to 76% at 0.36: whoever reached the simulation stayed to the finale. The simulation holds. The loss is at
  the front.
- Tail, 81% to 86% (0.31 to 0.26): chapter 08 "the decision" (starts at about 549 s, 81%): policy discussion after the strike.

## 5. Recommendations (ranked by expected impact)

1. Pull retention curves for every long video. Evidence: `studio/youtube.py` line 319 loops `out["videos"][:10]`, videos
   sorted by 28-day views. Shorts occupy the top 10; 003 to 006 (6 to 33 views) never get a curve, so C2 (hold at 11%) and
   every leak read for the judged episodes is blind. Change it to pull the long videos first (all videos whose episode
   `metadata.json` has a `youtube_id`, then fill with the top Shorts). Cost: about 6 extra API queries a day. Impact:
   decides whether the 2026-11-18 to 11-20 experiments can be judged at all. Not edited here (code, not an agent file).
2. Hold every episode-making rule constant until 2026-11-20. Evidence: the three running experiments (moving banner and
   history early on 004, photoreal thumbnails on 004, the 2026-11-10 guide on 006) are due 11-18, 11-18 and about 11-20, and
   the sample is 6 to 33 views. 003 to 005 were produced before the guide. Impact: a clean read.
3. Make the next long video the broad one. Evidence: Know Better 133K at 387 vph (685 subs), Spotlight 118K at 194 vph,
   The Nuclear Clock 226K for "Entire Arsenal", against our 007 carrier concept which has the channel's top Short
   (1,206). Full nuclear war, minute by minute, globe only, with the Short slots cut from named phases (first 6 minutes,
   the submarines, the first hour). Expected impact: highest on impressions, which is our binding constraint.
4. Cut Shorts by stake, not by format. Evidence: of the 7 Shorts above 400 views, 6 name a military or strategic stake
   (carrier group, interceptors, Putin's calls, mountain site twice, four warheads) and 1 is a city ring-by-ring. The 2 Shorts
   under 25 views are a generic missile timeline and a farmland burst. Keep craft rules 19 to 22;
   when a window can hit a military target or open on a question, take it. Small sample; treat as a test on 008 and 009.
5. Keep the first 80 seconds free of inventories and static cards. Evidence: 001 loses 20 points over a 19 s warhead card
   and 25 s region shot (46 to 91 s); 002 loses 34 points over a 14 s text card, chapter card and energy statistics
   (37 to 85 s). This is already rule 3 and the opening rules; no rewrite, just confirm on the 004 to 006 curves when
   recommendation 1 ships.
6. Move chapters 07 and 08 style history out of the last 10%. Evidence: 001 falls 0.23 to 0.09 over the last 20%; 002 falls
   0.31 to 0.26 over 81% to 86% when history and "the decision" arrive after the simulation. Already in CLAUDE.md. Check
   that 003 onward end on the day after (a scripted ending check against `script.md` headers, zero cost).
7. Test one Short-to-long bridge. Evidence: RELATED_VIDEO and YT_CHANNEL viewers watch about 3.5 minutes per view against
   0.35 for Shorts (10 times), but only 111 of 6,823 views (1.6%). The pill and the description link exist. Test on 008:
   put the long video's title, not "FULL SIMULATION ON THE CHANNEL", in the last-3-second pill. Measure RELATED_VIDEO views
   per 1,000 Shorts views (today 18) on the next scorecard. Do not apply until recommendation 2's date passes; log it as
   its own experiment.
8. Do not draw packaging conclusions from variant a to c yet. Evidence: no variant dimension in the export; the only
   sizeable sample is 001 (1,465 impressions, 2.4%). Measure next: record the live variant id per episode in
   `metadata.json` so CTR can be split by concept once any concept reaches 500 impressions.
