# Episode 003: North Korea strikes the US West Coast, minute by minute

- Topic: a simulated North Korean nuclear attack on the US West Coast, built from published models. It has not happened.
- Angle: what North Korea actually has (Hwasong-17/18/19 ICBMs, warhead estimates), what US missile defence (GMD, 44 interceptors) can and cannot do, and the roughly 30 to 35 minute flight from launch to impact.
- Why now: promised on episode 002's end slate; North Korean ICBM tests and the US West Coast are a top search pairing. Do not set the crisis on a real upcoming date.
- Hook moment: the detection of the launch, then the clock: about half an hour from launch to impact, with the interceptor engagement window in between.
- Hero strike: one Hwasong-class ICBM warhead on a West Coast target (scenario-designer to choose, e.g. Seattle or San Francisco), numbers from the scenario engine only.
- Thumbnail number: the flight time in minutes (from scenario.computed.json).
- Next episode for the end slate: One 800-kiloton airburst over one US city, street by street.
- Lane: great-power war and flashpoints (Korea); nuclear war and weapons.
