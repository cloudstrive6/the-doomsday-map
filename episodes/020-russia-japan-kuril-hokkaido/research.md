- Suggestion: open from real material (A2, A3: the August 2026 visit and missile firings), then label the break from reality ("here the simulation begins").

- Suggestion: Russia's nuclear step is a low-yield, non-strategic weapon (A20 notes about 1,794 non-strategic warheads, about 767 of them naval) used as a "de-escalation" demonstration or limited strike, justified inside Russian doctrine (A21) as defending territory it counts as its own. The engine, not this file, supplies yield, rings and flight times. Candidate delivery: Oniks or Kalibr from Kunashir or from a submarine in the Okhotsk Sea, or an air-launched missile from a bomber over the Sea of Okhotsk.

## D. Figures the script must NOT use

- "Russia is blockading Hokkaido" or "Russia fired missiles at Japan" (false: the August 2026 missiles were fired in drills near Russian-held islands at imaginary targets, A3).
- "Japan and Russia are at war" or "technically still at war" without context. The 1956 declaration ended the state of war; what is missing is a peace treaty (B5).
- "Putin's visit was the first by any Russian leader" (Medvedev went to Kunashir on 2010-11-01, B8; Putin's was the first by a Russian president to Iturup).
- "Hokkaido is 7 km from Russia" or similar single numbers: the closest disputed islet is about 3.7 km from Cape Nosappu, Kunashir is about 16 to 25 km, and Sakhalin is about 42 km across the Soya Strait (A11, A13). Always say which.
- The drill size "60 warships, 30 aircraft and 13,000 personnel" (seen only in a search snippet of a Moscow Times article dated 2026-08-05, which also contradicts the dates of the Putin visit; not opened, SINGLE-SOURCE).
- "A Bastion has a 500 km range" or any single Bastion range (sources give about 300 to 450 km, A15; Russia claims hits beyond 300 km, A3).
- "Russia has 6,000 nuclear weapons" (inventory is about 5,420 including about 1,020 retired; military stockpile about 4,400; deployed strategic about 1,796, A20). Do not say Russia keeps nuclear weapons on the Kurils: no source supports it (all non-strategic warheads are declared in central storage).
- "Russia has about 1,500 nuclear warheads aimed at Japan" or any "aimed at Japan" count: unsourced.
- "New START is still in force" (it expired 2026-02-05, A22).
- "The US is treaty-bound to defend the Kuril Islands" or "Article 5 covers the Northern Territories" (Article 5 covers "territories under the administration of Japan"; Russia administers the islands and no opened source says Washington would treat them as covered, A28).
