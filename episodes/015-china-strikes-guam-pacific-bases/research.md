Compiled 2026-11-22. Every claim the script uses is listed with its sources. Two reputable, independent sources per fact is the rule; single-source items are marked SINGLE-SOURCE and must be softened in the script ("about", "one estimate puts it at") or cut. Where sources disagree, the range is given with who says what. Simulation assumptions are listed separately and must be labelled SIMULATION on screen. Source notes: URLs listed below were opened on 2026-11-22 unless a row says "carried from 002" (opened by the desk on 2026-11-05, not reopened) or "search summary only" (the page returned an error or a binary PDF when opened; the figure came from the search result summary and should be treated as SINGLE-SOURCE until reopened). Flight times, blast and fallout numbers are NOT in this file: they come only from `python -m studio scenario`.

**Framing check, read first.** As of 2026-11-22 China has NOT struck Guam, Kadena, Yokosuka or any US base, and no missile has been fired at US forces (A1). What has changed in the last 60 days and is worth knowing: (1) from about 2026-11-02 China ran a large UNANNOUNCED naval deployment in the Western Pacific: Taiwan's National Security Council counted more than 100 navy and coast guard vessels on 2026-11-03 and about 70 by 2026-11-13, from all three PLA theatre commands, with the carrier Liaoning group active east of the Philippines; ISW assessed it could simulate isolating Taiwan and keeping foreign forces out; Beijing called the carrier activity routine training (A2). A Taiwanese official told Reuters a Chinese submarine launched an ICBM, which is NOT independently confirmed and must not be used (D8). (2) The US-Japan exercise Keen Sword 27 ran 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-29 with about 56,000 personnel (A3). (3) Valiant Shield 2026 ran 2026-06-22 to 2026-07-01 across Japan, Guam and the Northern Marianas, and a US Army Dark Eagle hypersonic missile was photographed on Guam (A22). (4) Trump and Xi met in Shenzhen on 2026-11-19 at APEC; the Chinese readout has Trump saying the US "does not support Taiwan independence" (A4). The honest hook is "this has not happened, but the forces are moving", and the strike itself must be labelled SIMULATION. Upcoming real dates the episode must not collide with: Taiwan's local elections on 2026-11-28 (carried from 002), the PLA's usual November to December drill season, the anniversary of Pearl Harbor on 2026-12-07, and the Guam missile-defense system's expected operational date of late 2027 (A21). The script must give no real date for the simulated strike. Re-check the news on the day of upload, especially the unannounced PLA deployment, which could turn into a named drill.

## C. Simulation assumptions (SIMULATION tag, stated as assumptions in the script)

To be written by the scenario designer. The suggestions below are the research desk's suggestions only, not decisions.

- SUGGESTION: no calendar date on screen, use "minutes after launch" and "hour one". The crisis must not read as a prediction or sit on 2026-11-28 (Taiwan elections) or 2026-12-07 (Pearl Harbor anniversary).
- SUGGESTION: hero strike is one DF-26-class missile on Andersen AFB (13.576 N, 144.924 E). The 002 engine run used a simulated mainland site at 25.0 N, 114.5 E (3,426 km to Guam). Launch sites are simulated and must be labelled so. Use whatever flight time the engine returns.
- SUGGESTION: warhead choice is the designer's labelled decision. Options with published backing: conventional (most DF-26s have conventional missions, A6) or a low-yield nuclear warhead under 10 kt (Pentagon, A10). A conventional strike is the more defensible opener (CNAS and the Pettyjohn games open with conventional strikes on Guam, A25); a nuclear variant should be framed as the threshold the episode is about (A27).
- SUGGESTION: secondary simulated strikes on Kadena (about 2,280 km from Andersen, A13) and Yokosuka can come from DF-17 or DF-26 sites, as in 002. Keep counts small and state them as assumptions.
- SUGGESTION: "what survives the first hour" must lean on published studies for the effect on air power: Stimson (about 12 days of lost fighter operations in Japan, nearly 2 days at Guam, A19) and RAND (A20, A21). Do not invent a count of aircraft destroyed.
- SUGGESTION: defence assumptions: say Guam has Patriot, THAAD and a still-developing Aegis system expected operational late 2027 (A23); say whether the simulation lets any missile through and why, as a choice, not a fact.
- SUGGESTION: aftermath chapter: bases closed, tankers grounded in Japan (A19), carriers at sea as the fallback (B1 shows the carriers survived Pearl Harbor because they were away), the nuclear threshold decision (A27, A28). End on the aftermath, not on history.
- SUGGESTION: no casualty figure for Guam's roughly 170,000 residents (A16) unless a published study supplies one. No named people.
- No SLCM-N option (A28).

## D. Figures the script must NOT use

- "China has attacked or fired at Guam" or "a strike on Guam is imminent" (false as of 2026-11-22; only an unannounced naval deployment, A1, A2).
- "A Chinese submarine launched an ICBM in November 2026" (one unnamed Taiwanese official told Reuters; not independently confirmed, A2).
- "China's DF-26 force has 400 or 500 launchers" (the 500 is the Pentagon's count of IRBM MISSILES, not launchers; launchers are about 250 to 300, A6). Also "all DF-26s are nuclear" (FAS says most have conventional missions, A6).
- A DF-26 range of 2,000 miles or 5,000 km without attribution: sources give about 4,000 km (CSIS, FAS) or nearly 2,500 miles (Pentagon 2017, via Task & Purpose). Use "about 4,000 km, roughly 2,500 miles".
- "Guam is 1,500 miles from China" (a common mixed-up figure; our checks and sources give about 2,700 to 3,100 km, about 1,700 to 1,900 miles, A12).
- "Guam has 30,000 or 5,000 troops" as a current number (no 2026 figure confirmed; the 6,000 is from 2016 to 2017, A16). Note also that the Marine relocation to Guam was not verified in this research.
- "The DF-26 can hit a moving carrier reliably" (the anti-ship variant is unproven against moving ships, CIMSEC, A7). "Carrier killer" is a nickname.
- "Stimson says Guam would be shut for two weeks" (Stimson says about 12 days for Japan bases and nearly 2 days for Guam, A19).
- Aircraft lost at Pearl Harbor as one exact figure (sources range from about 180 to over 300 destroyed or damaged, B1).
- "Dark Eagle is based on Guam" as settled fact (PACOM confirmed photos taken on Guam during Valiant Shield, not that it remains, A24). Its range ranges 2,175 miles to 3,500 km by source.
- "North Korea fired missiles at Guam in 2017" (it threatened and never launched toward Guam, B5). The 3,356 km flight distance is single-source.
- "Trump said the US would not defend Taiwan" or "Beijing and Washington agreed on Taiwan at APEC" (only a Chinese readout quoting a long-standing position, A4).
- "Japan's 7th Fleet" or "several US carriers in Japan" (one forward-deployed carrier, USS George Washington, A18; fleet is the US 7th Fleet).
- Any blast, burn, fallout, flight time or casualty number not from `scenario.computed.json`.
- The CSIS wargame losses as a prediction ("the US will lose two carriers"): they are wargame outcomes (A26).
