# Episode 019: US versus China, the first week of a Pacific war: who runs out of missiles first

- Topic: a simulated first week of a US-China war in the Western Pacific, told through the missile stockpiles. Real data first (published inventories of US long-range munitions such as JASSM, LRASM, Tomahawk, SM-6 and PAC-3 interceptors, annual production rates, China's DF-21/DF-26 and cruise-missile inventory estimates, CSIS and RAND wargame findings), then one labelled simulation of the first days, then what the map looks like after. Built from published models. It has not happened.

- Thumbnail number: days until the magazines are empty, or the missile count, from research/scenario only.
