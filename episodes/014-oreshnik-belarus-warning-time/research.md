Compiled 2026-11-21. Every claim the script uses is listed with its sources. Two reputable, independent sources per fact is the rule; single-source items are marked SINGLE-SOURCE and must be softened in the script ("about", "one estimate puts it at", "Russia says") or cut. **Framing check, read first.** As of 2026-11-21 an Oreshnik has NEVER been fired at a NATO country or from Belarus. It has been fired at Ukraine three times, all from Russian soil: Dnipro on 2024-11-21 (A2), Lviv region on the night of 2026-01-08 to 2026-01-09 (A8) and the Kyiv region (Bila Tserkva) on 2026-05-24 (A9). The Belarus deployment is real in the sense that Russia's Defence Ministry announced combat duty on 2025-12-30 and Lukashenko said the system arrived on 2025-12-17 (A10), but the launcher count and exact base are not officially confirmed (A11, A12), and Jamestown argues Minsk has no control over launch orders (A13). Three things from the last 60 days the script can use or must respect. (1) Peace talks are live: US, Ukrainian and Russian delegations met in Abu Dhabi on 2026-11-10 and 2026-11-11 (first trilateral round since February, a prisoner swap agreed in principle, no ceasefire), US envoys met Putin in Moscow in the week of 2026-11-12, and Ukraine met US and European envoys online on 2026-11-16 with a Berlin meeting planned and no date for the next round (A27). (2) On 2026-11-11 the New York Times reported Ukrainian soldiers saying their "Lima" electronic-warfare system pushed two Oreshniks off target on 2026-05-24 (A9, soldiers' claim, unverified). (3) Early November 2026 saw hacked Ukrainian and Russian emergency apps push fake nuclear and "Oreshnik deployed" alerts (CNN, 2026-11-02, https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/02/europe/hacked-russian-ukrainian-emergency-apps-intl, blocked on fetch, headline from search listing only: SINGLE-SOURCE). One unverified item: a report dated 2026-11-18 that Moscow is planning new Oreshnik strikes on Kyiv after an attack on the Crimean Bridge traces to a single X account; do not use it. Real dates the episode must not collide with: 2026-11-21 (today) is the second anniversary of the Dnipro strike; 2026-12-17 and 2026-12-30 are the first anniversaries of the Belarus combat-duty announcements; the next trilateral talks round and the Berlin "Coalition of the Willing" meeting have no date. ## C. Simulation assumptions (SIMULATION tag, stated as assumptions in the script)
