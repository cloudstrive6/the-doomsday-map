# Episode 013: Taiwan goes dark: a cyber attack on the grid and the chip fabs, hour by hour

- Topic: a simulated cyber and sabotage attack on Taiwan's power grid, undersea cables and chip fabs, hour by hour from the first outage. Real history first (Ukraine grid attacks of 2015 and 2016, the 2022 Viasat hack, the 2018 TSMC virus outage, the 2023 Matsu cable cuts, Taiwan's 2022 grid blackout from a non-hostile fault, the roughly 11 days of LNG reserves), then one clearly labelled simulation built from published models, then the day after on the map. Ends on the aftermath (what stays dark, what the fabs cannot make), never history or statistics. It has not happened.
- Angle: infrastructure lane. Taiwan makes most of the world's leading-edge chips and imports almost all its energy; the question the episode answers out loud is how long the island and the world can run once the lights go out. Differs from 002 (blockade): no ships, a cyber and grid war.
- Why now: backlog item 6; follows 002's audience; our "11 days of gas" Short is 267 views. Hypothetical framing, no real date, no casualty figure unless a published study supplies it.
- Hook moment: the first substation or fab district going dark on the map inside 8 s, under the banner, on moving footage; clock at hour 0.
- Hero strike: the scenario-designer decides whether any kinetic or EMP element belongs; default is no nuclear element (lane 3). If used, numbers from the engine only.
- Thumbnail number: the days of energy reserve (confirm against research.md, one number in title and thumbnail).
- Next episode for the end slate: Russia's Oreshnik hypersonic missile, what it can reach from Belarus (no date).
- Lane: EMP, infrastructure and cyber attacks on nations.
