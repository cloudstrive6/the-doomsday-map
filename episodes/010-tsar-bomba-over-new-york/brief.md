# Episode 010: One Tsar Bomba-class warhead over New York, ring by ring

- Topic: a simulated single 50-megaton airburst (the size of the real Tsar Bomba test of 30 October 1961) over New York City, shown ring by ring from the scenario engine, then a modern 800-kiloton-class warhead beside it for scale. Built from published effects models (Glasstone and Dolan, NUKEMAP-style published fits) and the real 1961 test data. It has not happened and is not a prediction.
- Angle: real history and data first (the 1961 test over Novaya Zemlya: yield, burst height, what was measured, the shockwave circling the Earth, why the design was cut from 100 Mt to 50 Mt, why nobody deployed it), then one clearly labelled simulation on the New York map, ring by ring, then the day after on the map. Last chapter is the simulation's aftermath, never a history or statistics chapter. No death toll unless a published study supplies it.
- Why now: scale content is the largest nuclear audience on YouTube (Science Time "True Scale of a 100 Megaton Nuke on New York" 3.5M; "True Scale of Modern Nuclear Weapons" 27.8M; Visualiser 3D London 881K). Ranked item 3 in data/ideas.md after 008 and 009 shipped. Hypothetical framing, no real date, no named living people as victims.
- Hook moment: the flash over Manhattan under the banner inside 8 s, clock at the burst, "the fireball is bigger than the island".
- Hero strike: one 50 Mt airburst over lower Manhattan (scenario-designer sets burst height; all numbers from the engine only), then one modern ~800 kt warhead for scale.
- Thumbnail number: "50 MEGATONS".
