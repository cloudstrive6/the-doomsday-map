# Episode 008: Full nuclear war, minute by minute

- Topic: a simulated full-scale US and Russian nuclear exchange, minute by minute, on globe views, built from published models (Princeton Plan A, 2019; Toon et al. 2019 and 2022 nuclear winter studies; FAS and SIPRI arsenal estimates; real ICBM and SLBM flight times). It has not happened.
- Angle: real history and data first (the real 1983 and 1995 close calls, how launch-on-warning works, real flight times), then one clearly labelled simulation: the first 6 minutes, the submarines, the first hour; last chapter is the simulation's day after, never a statistics or history chapter. Three Shorts cut from named phases.
- Why now: the broad premise is the fastest riser in lane (Know Better "What If Full Nuclear War Happened? Minute by Minute" 133K, Spotlight "The First 60 Minutes of Nuclear War" 118K, The Nuclear Clock "Entire Arsenal" 226K). Our slate has no broad episode. Framed as a hypothetical, no real date, no casualty figure unless a published study supplies it (cite the study).
- Hook moment: the first flash over a real target under the banner inside 8 s, clock at minute one.
- Hero strike: the first warheads landing on a real target (scenario-designer picks; numbers from the engine only).
- Thumbnail number: the minutes (for example "30 MINUTES"), from the scenario flight times.
- Next episode for the end slate: Russia attacks the Baltic, the first 72 hours (no date).
- Lane: nuclear war and weapons.
