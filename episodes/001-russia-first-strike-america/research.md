## C. Simulation assumptions (SIMULATION tag, stated as assumptions in the script)

- The war begins after a conventional clash on NATO's eastern flank escalates and Russia decides to strike first at America's nuclear forces before they can be used ("counterforce"), the opening logic of Plan A's second phase.
- First wave: about 800 warheads at roughly 500 targets: two warheads on each of the 450 Minuteman silos is the open-literature planning norm, plus launch control centres, the three bomber bases, two submarine bases, early-warning radars and command nodes (Offutt, Peterson and Cheyenne Mountain, the Pentagon and Washington). Figures are the simulation's, not a forecast.
- ICBMs fly from the divisions at Kozelsk, Tatishchevo, Dombarovsky, Uzhur, Yoshkar-Ola, Teykovo, Nizhny Tagil, Novosibirsk, Barnaul and Irkutsk; SLBMs from the Barents Sea and Sea of Okhotsk bastions. Flight times from studio/scenario.py (about 26 to 30 minutes).
- Offutt is hit by one 800-kiloton ground burst (the yield class of Russia's heaviest silo-based warheads), because its command post is underground. Effects from the engine: fireball about 1.1 km radius, 20 psi 1.0 km, 5 psi 5.0 km, third-degree burns about 9.8 km, 1 psi 13.6 km, heavy local fallout downwind (prevailing wind from the west-northwest in November). Downtown Omaha is about 15 km north-north-west of the runway; Bellevue is inside the 5 psi ring.
- The US launches its ICBMs on warning before the Russian warheads arrive (launch under attack), so most silos are empty when hit; the exchange then follows Plan A's countervalue phase. Script says Plan A's totals, not ours.
- Time of day: 03:40 Central Standard Time, chosen so the globe shows night over North America and the script can say most Americans are asleep.
