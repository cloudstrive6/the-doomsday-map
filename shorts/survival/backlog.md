## 1. First 15 Minutes After a Nuclear Warning (PILOT, folder 001)

- **Why it ranks first:** the channel's core subject turned into the one thing everyone should know; it answers the
  question every nuclear video raises ("what would I do?"). Full fact sheet in `001-first-15-minutes-nuclear-warning/facts.md`.
- **Hook numbers:** 10 MINUTES (fallout) and 24 HOURS (stay inside).
- **Fatal mistake:** getting in the car to flee or to collect family.
- **Rule 01:** GET INSIDE the nearest sturdy building, then go down or to the middle.
- **Steps (device per item):**
  1. Get inside: PF gauge needle swings CAR 2, HOUSE 3, BASEMENT 10 to 20, BUILDING CORE 100.
  2. Basement or middle, away from walls and roof: dose counter rolls 100% to 10% to 1% as the icon moves inward.
  3. Close windows and doors, fans and air con off: state flips VENT: ON to OFF, DAMPER: OPEN to CLOSED.
  4. Stay at least 24 hours: fill bar 0 H to 24 H.
  5. Stay tuned on a battery or hand-crank radio: CELL bars drop to 0 while RADIO: RECEIVING lights up.
- **Clock framing:** FIRST 15 MINUTES (running clock), with a smaller "10:00 TO FALLOUT" pill.
- **Sources:** READY-RAD "you will have 10 minutes or more to find an adequate shelter before fallout arrives";
  "Remain in the most protective location (basement or center of a large building) for the first 24 hours".
  CDC-IN "Staying inside for at least 24 hours can protect you and your family". CDC-FAQ "Cars do not provide good
  protection from radioactive material." LLNL Table 1: Vehicle PF 1.7-2, Wood Frame House 2-3, Basement 10-20.

## 2. Never Drive Away From a Nuclear Blast

- **Hook numbers:** PF 2 (a car only halves your dose) against PF 10 to 20 (basement); 50+ MPH upper winds.
- **Fatal mistake:** jumping in the car to outrun the fallout.
- **Rule 01:** Leave the car. Get into the nearest building.
- **Steps:**
  1. A car is not a shelter: two panels flip CAR: NOT SAFE, BUILDING: SAFER (dose meter half vs one tenth).
  2. You cannot outrun it: speedometer needle at your speed while a wind gauge passes 50 MPH.
  3. Everyone else is driving too: lane counter of cars jams, ROUTE: CLEAR to ROUTE: BLOCKED.
  4. Wait for an official route: state flip EVACUATION: NOT YET to ROUTE GIVEN.
- **Clock framing:** 10 MINUTES TO SHELTER.
- **Sources:** CDC-FAQ "Cars do not provide good protection from radioactive material."; "Stay inside where you are
  until an evacuation order is given." LLNL: "Upper atmospheric winds often travel at high speeds (>50 mph), making it
  difficult to 'outrun' the fallout cloud"; "attempts to evacuate a large population before the fallout arrives will
  likely result in traffic jams. As vehicles are inadequate shelters..."; Table 1 Vehicle PF 1.7-2. HI-EMA
  (https://dod.hawaii.gov/hiema/faqs-related-to-the-ballistic-missile-false-alert/): get out of the car and find
  shelter or lie flat.

## 3. The Room That Protects You Most From Fallout

- **Hook numbers:** 1/100 of the dose (core of a big building, PF 100) against 1/2 (a car).
- **Fatal mistake:** sheltering on the top floor or in an outer ground-floor room by a window.
- **Rule 01:** Go down or go to the middle. Distance from the roof and outer walls is protection.
- **Steps:**
  1. Basement, against a wall in the corner: PF gauge to 10 to 20.
  2. Middle floors, centre of a large concrete or brick building: counter rolls to PF 100.
  3. Stay away from the roof and outer walls: cutaway building where outer rooms dim red and the core glows green.
  4. Know the bad shelters: stamp INADEQUATE slams on car, mobile home, single-storey wood house without basement.
- **Clock framing:** 10 MINUTES TO SHELTER.
- **Sources:** LLNL Table 1 (Underground 3 feet 5,000; Wood Frame House 2-3; Basement 10-20; Vehicle 1.7-2;
  Apartment upper stories 100; lower stories 10) and "someone in the core of the building halfway up ... would have a
  PF of 100 and receive only 1/100 (or 1%) of the outdoor exposure". FEMA-72: "the best shelter location is in the
  center of a large building of heavy construction"; "a dose reduction factor of 200 indicates that a person in that
  area would receive 1/200th of the dose". FEMA-TALK: "even the basement of a wood frame structure provides adequate
  protection". CDC-IN: "Radiation settles on the outside of buildings."

## 4. Missile Alert on Your Phone: What to Do Now

- **Hook numbers:** 15 TO 30 MINUTES (US planning figure from warning to detonation); the alert sounds TWICE.
- **Fatal mistake:** going to the window or outside to look. (Second beat: trusting an unverified app alert, see the
  2026-11-01 fake alerts.)
- **Rule 01:** Basement or central room, NOW, away from windows and doors.
- **Steps:**
  1. Know a real alert: phone mock-up (generic, not a real OS) flips NORMAL to NATIONAL ALERT, sound icon pulses x2.
  2. Get down or to the centre: countdown pill runs while a floor-plan arrow moves inward.
  3. Away from glass: window panel flips INTACT to SHATTERED with a distance meter.
  4. No building near: posture icon flips STANDING to FLAT, hands over head.
  5. Stay inside and check official radio or TV: signal bars, label OFFICIAL SOURCE: CONFIRMED.
- **Clock framing:** 15 MINUTES TO IMPACT, labelled "PLANNING FIGURE, FEMA".
- **Sources:** FEMA-72: "If the warning is due to a nuclear-armed ballistic missile, SLTT jurisdictions may only have
  15-30 minutes between the warning and the detonation"; template alert "Get inside a basement or central room of a
  sturdy building NOW and stay away from windows and doors." READY-ALERTS: "WEAs look like text messages but are
  designed to get your attention with a unique sound and vibration repeated twice"; "WEAs are not affected by network
  congestion". HI-EMA: "approximately only 12 to 15 minutes would be left to warn the public". Japan, national
  J-Alert advice as published by Fukuoka City (https://www.city.fukuoka.lg.jp/shimin/bousai/bousai/missile_english.html):
  "lie down on the ground and cover your head"; "Lie down in the center of the building, staying away from windows or
  doors."

## 5. Fallout's 7-10 Rule: Why the First Day Matters

- **Hook numbers:** 7 HOURS = 1/10; 2 DAYS = 1/100.
- **Fatal mistake:** leaving the shelter in the first few hours "to check".
- **Rule 01:** The danger falls fast, so wait it out inside.
- **Steps:**
  1. Factor of 10 for every 7 times the time: dose-rate counter rolls 100 to 10 at H+7.
  2. About 2 days: counter rolls to 1.
  3. Over 80 percent gone in the first day: fill bar drains on a 24 H track.
  4. Stay at least 24 hours: state flip SHELTER: HOLD.
  5. Leave only when officials say so: state flip to ALL CLEAR (from officials, never from the clock).
- **Clock framing:** FIRST 48 HOURS.
- **Sources:** REMM: "The level of fallout decays quickly, roughly by a factor of 10 for every 7-fold increase in
  time"; hypothetical 10 kT example: about 20% at 3 hours, 10% at 8 hours, 1% at 48 hours. FEMA-TALK (Buddemeier):
  "radiation decays rapidly - over 80 percent the first day"; "Much of the danger from radiation is reduced after the
  first 24 hours." READY-RAD: first 24 hours. Note: the name "7-10 rule" is a civil-defence rule of thumb; REMM states
  the rule without the name. Say "a rule of thumb used by planners".

## 6. Caught Outside When the Flash Hits

- **Hook numbers:** 90% (outer clothes removed) and 10 MINUTES (to get inside).
- **Fatal mistake:** staying outside to watch the cloud.
- **Rule 01:** Lie face down, then get inside fast.
- **Steps:**
  1. Lie face down, protect skin: posture flip, heat bar sweeps over.
  2. When the blast has passed, nearest building: 10-minute pill, door icon OPEN.
  3. Outer layer off, into a bag: contamination bar drops by 90%.
  4. Wash with soap and shampoo, no conditioner: state flip CONDITIONER: NO.
  5. Clean clothes from a drawer: tick.
- **Clock framing:** FIRST 10 MINUTES.
- **Sources:** READY-RAD: "lie face down to protect exposed skin from the heat and flying debris"; "Removing your outer
  layer of clothing can remove up to 90% of radioactive material." CDC-DECON: "Do not use conditioner because it will
  cause radioactive material to stick to your hair." REMM: "During daylight, flash blindness does not persist for
  greater than about 2 minutes, but lasts generally seconds". LLNL: "Avoid outdoor exposure during the first few
  minutes and hours after the fallout arrives".

## 7. The EU's New 72-Hour Rule: 6 Basics

- **Hook numbers:** 72 HOURS on your own; 6 BASICS (water, food, warmth, hygiene, medicine, information).
- **Fatal mistake:** relying on card and phone payments when the network is down.
- **Rule 01:** Water first: at least 3 litres per person per day (UK and NZ figure).
- **Steps:**
  1. Water: bottles fill to 9 L per person for 3 days.
  2. Food that needs no cooking: day counter 0 to 3.
  3. Light and heat: torch beam on, candle icon crossed (UK: torches are safer than candles).
  4. Battery radio and a charged power bank: battery gauge holds at 100%.
  5. Cash in small notes and coins: coin counter fills while PAYMENTS: OFFLINE stays red.
- **Clock framing:** 72 HOURS ON YOUR OWN.
- **Sources:** EU-72 (SWD(2026) 826 final, 2026-10-30): "Promote at least 72 hours (three days) of self-sufficiency";
  six basics "(water, food, warmth, hygiene, medicine, information and communication)"; "A reserve of cash, including
  small notes and coins, for essential household purchases in case digital payment systems fail"; "radio powered by
  batteries, fully charged power banks". News of the 2026-11-03 launch:
  https://civil-protection-humanitarian-aid.ec.europa.eu/news-stories/news/commission-takes-new-steps-prepare-europeans-growing-crisis-risks-2026-11-03_en
  UK-READY: "3 litres per person per day for drinking"; "Torches are safer than candles."
- **Overlap warning:** the reference reel did "4 items for 72 hours". Use different devices and our own script.

## 8. Don't Go Get Your Kids. Here's Why.

- **Hook numbers:** THE FIRST HOUR (the worst exposure); 24 HOURS (stay put).
- **Fatal mistake:** driving across town to the school.
- **Rule 01:** Stay where you are. Schools shelter children.
- **Steps:**
  1. Schools have plans: state flip SCHOOL: SHELTERING IN PLACE.
  2. Going out exposes you both: two dose meters rise together on a map route.
  3. Text, do not call: CALL FAILED flips to TEXT DELIVERED.
  4. Agree the plan today: meeting place and out-of-area contact tick in.
- **Clock framing:** FIRST HOUR AFTER FALLOUT.
- **Sources:** CDC-FAQ: "Going outside to get loved ones could expose you and them to dangerous levels of
  radiation." UK-RAD: do not go to collect children if told to stay indoors; schools and nurseries will be told to
  stay where they are. FEMA-TALK: "Do not try to reunite with family members before taking shelter." LLNL: "The most
  significant exposures from fallout occur in the first hour after fallout arrives."

## 9. Iodine Pills: What They Can and Cannot Do

- **Hook numbers:** 1 ORGAN (thyroid only); 24 HOURS (one dose).
- **Fatal mistake:** taking pills or iodised salt on your own instead of sheltering.
- **Rule 01:** Shelter is the protection. Take KI only when officials tell you.
- **Steps:**
  1. Thyroid only: body outline where only the thyroid lights.
  2. Radioactive iodine only: filter icon stops one particle type, others pass.
  3. Timing: timeline bar from 24 h before to 4 h after (CDC).
  4. Who: under 40, pregnant or breastfeeding: age gauge.
  5. Never table salt: stamp NOT A SUBSTITUTE.
- **Clock framing:** 24 HOURS.
- **Sources:** CDC-KI: "KI protects only the thyroid"; "Do not take KI unless you are instructed by public health or
  emergency response officials or a healthcare provider"; "within 24 hours before or 4 hours after exposure";
  "One dose of KI offers protection for 24 hours"; "only recommended for people under 40 and women who are pregnant
  or breastfeeding"; "Do not use table salt or foods that contain iodine as a substitute". UK-RAD: "most effective
  between 24 hours before and 2 hours after exposure ... can still be useful up to 8 hours after". **Figures differ**
  (CDC 4 h, UK 2 h best and up to 8 h); say "take it only when officials say, at the time they say".

## 10. Decontaminate Yourself in 4 Steps

- **Hook numbers:** 90% (outer layer) and 4 STEPS (CDC's numbered infographic; no official time figure exists).
- **Fatal mistake:** locking the door on someone covered in fallout. (Officials: let them in.)
- **Rule 01:** Outer layer off first, then wash.
- **Steps:**
  1. Clothes off carefully, into a sealed bag, away from people and pets: contamination bar falls 90%.
  2. Shower, soap and shampoo, no conditioner, do not scrub: state flips.
  3. No shower: wipe face and hands, blow nose, wipe eyelids and ears: checklist ticks.
  4. Clean clothes from a closet or drawer: tick, CLEAN.
- **Clock framing:** FIRST HOUR INSIDE.
- **Sources:** CDC-DECON: "Taking off your outer layer of clothing can remove up to 90% of radioactive material.";
  "Do not scald, scrub, or scratch your skin." FEMA-72: "Contaminated material can easily be caught in the collar of
  shirts, top of shoes around the ankles". FEMA-TALK: "let them in! Decontamination (removing the fallout) is quick
  and simple"; "will not endanger their lives".

## 11. EMP Myths vs What Officials Say

- **Hook numbers:** 72 HOURS (power back outside the worst zone); 0 direct health threat.
- **Fatal mistake:** believing every device is dead forever, so not keeping a battery radio and power bank.
- **Rule 01:** Keep battery devices unplugged and ready; the towers are the weak point.
- **Steps:**
  1. EMP itself does not hurt people: MYTH / FACT card flip.
  2. Phones not on charge may be unaffected: plug icon disconnects, phone stays ON.
  3. Cars may stall: state flip ENGINE: RUNNING to STALLED (no claim about restarting).
  4. Many devices only need breakers reset: breaker flips TRIPPED to RESET.
  5. Power back in most areas outside the light damage zone within about 72 hours: gauge.
- **Clock framing:** FIRST 72 HOURS.
- **Sources:** REMM EMP: "The EMP associated with a nuclear detonation poses no direct health threat"; cell phones "If
  they are not connected to electrical power supplies during the electromagnetic pulse (EMP) they may not be
  affected"; "stalling of vehicles"; "Electronic devices may only require resetting switches and circuit breakers."
  FEMA-72: "Generators have proven very robust against the EMP threat."; "Towers are the most vulnerable part of a
  communications network"; power restored "outside the LDZ as well as in some areas in the LDZ within 72 hours".
- **Keep out:** high-altitude EMP "continent goes dark" claims, Faraday-cage product claims, car-restart claims (no
  official consumer source found).

## 12. How Much Water to Store (Officials' Numbers)

- **Hook numbers:** 3 LITRES per person per day; 6 MONTHS (replace self-filled water).
- **Fatal mistake:** storing water in old milk jugs.
- **Rule 01:** At least 3 litres per person per day, for at least 3 days.
- **Steps:**
  1. 9 litres per person for 3 days: four bottles fill one by one.
  2. Soft-drink bottles yes, milk containers no: state flip OK / NO.
  3. Self-filled water out every 6 months: calendar counter.
  4. Hidden reserve: toilet tank (not bowl) and water heater: tank gauge.
  5. Unsure water: rolling boil 1 full minute: timer.
- **Clock framing:** 72 HOURS WITHOUT TAP WATER.
- **Sources:** NZ Get Ready storing water (https://getready.govt.nz/assets/Uploads/documents/alternate-formats/large-print/pdf/22-storing-water-lp-en-oct23.pdf):
  "at least three litres of drinking water per person per day (at least nine litres per person for the three
  days)"; "Don't use plastic jugs or cardboard containers that have had milk in them." READY-WATER: "Store at least
  one gallon of water per person per day for several days"; "Water that has not been commercially bottled should be
  replaced every six months"; "bring water to a rolling boil for one full minute". Ready.gov nuclear detonation flyer
  (https://www.ready.gov/sites/default/files/2023-01/ready-gov_nuclear-detonation-safety_flyer1.pdf): "Water from your
  toilet tank (not bowl) and water heater is also safe."
- **Figures differ by country:** US 1 gallon (about 3.8 L); Canada "about four litres per person, per day"
  (https://www.canada.ca/en/services/policing/emergencies/preparedness/get-prepared/emergency-kits.html); UK and NZ
  3 L; Germany BBK 2 L for 10 days (secondary sources only, bbk.bund.de page not reached); Sweden "at least three to
  five litres" for a week (https://www.krisinformation.se/en/hazards-and-risks/home-preparedness/water-supply-in-a-crisis/);
  Finland about 2 L drinking. Use "about 3 litres, more if officials where you live say so".

## 13. Power Cut: What to Do in the First Hour

- **Hook numbers:** 4 HOURS (fridge), 48 HOURS (full freezer).
- **Fatal mistake:** opening the fridge and freezer to check.
- **Rule 01:** Keep the doors shut and unplug electronics.
- **Steps:**
  1. Fridge and freezer closed: two timers, 4 H and 48 H.
  2. Unplug appliances against the surge when power returns: plug flips IN to OUT.
  3. Torch, not candles: state flip.
  4. Food at 40 F (4 C) or more for 2 hours goes in the bin: thermometer.
  5. Save the outage number on paper (UK 105): paper card.
- **Clock framing:** FIRST HOUR IN THE DARK.
- **Sources:** READY-POWER: "The refrigerator will keep food cold for about four hours."; "A full freezer will keep
  the temperature for about 48 hours."; "Disconnect appliances and electronics to avoid damage from electrical
  surges."; "Throw away any food that has been exposed to temperatures 40 degrees or higher for two hours or more."
  UK-READY: report a power cut on 105 (England, Scotland, Wales); "Torches are safer than candles."
- **Overlap warning:** the reference reel was a blackout video with the freezer numbers. Lower in the queue; make it
  only after the nuclear topics prove the format, and lead with the surge and torch beats instead.

## 14. When the Phone Network Fails

- **Hook numbers:** 10 MINUTES (time for the US president to address the nation by EAS); alerts arrive even when
  calls do not.
- **Fatal mistake:** redialling again and again (drains the battery, jams the network).
- **Rule 01:** Text, do not call.
- **Steps:**
  1. Text, not call: CALL FAILED to TEXT DELIVERED.
  2. Battery or wind-up FM radio: dial needle sweeps to a station.
  3. Station frequencies written on paper: card flips in.
  4. Charged power bank: battery gauge.
  5. Emergency alerts still arrive: WEA badge NOT AFFECTED BY CONGESTION.
- **Clock framing:** FIRST 24 HOURS OFFLINE.
- **Sources:** READY-ALERTS: EAS "allows the president to address the nation within 10 minutes"; "WEAs are not
  affected by network congestion". UK-READY: "FM radio (battery / wind up)" and write down station frequencies.
  FCC/FEMA tips (https://www.fcc.gov/fcc-fema-emergency-tips, page blocked our fetcher with HTTP 403; wording from its
  search excerpt): "try text messaging"; "Limiting calls also conserves cell phone battery power." Re-check this page
  in a browser before scripting. FEMA-72: "Towers are the most vulnerable part of a communications network".

## 15. Cold Blackout: The Mistake That Kills

- **Hook numbers:** 20 FEET (generator from windows and doors); 95 F (hypothermia emergency).
- **Fatal mistake:** running a generator in the garage, or heating with the gas oven.
- **Rule 01:** Generators outdoors, at least 20 feet from windows, doors and attached garages.
- **Steps:**
  1. 20 feet out: distance meter.
  2. Never heat with a gas stove or oven: state flip OVEN: NOT A HEATER.
  3. Carbon monoxide has no smell: CO alarm flips SILENT to ALARM.
  4. Know hypothermia: thermometer falls below 95 F, symptom icons.
  5. Layers, one torch per person: tick list.
- **Clock framing:** FIRST NIGHT WITHOUT HEAT.
- **Sources:** READY-POWER: "Generators and fuel should always be used outdoors and at least 20 feet away from
  windows, doors and attached garages."; "Carbon monoxide is a colorless, odorless gas that can kill you, your family
  and pets." READY-WINTER: "A temperature below 95 degrees is an emergency."; never heat your home with a gas stove or
  oven. UK-READY: torches safer than candles.

## 16. Pets in a Fallout Shelter

- **Hook numbers:** 24 HOURS (keep pets in); 0 trips outside to find them.
- **Fatal mistake:** going outside to look for the pet.
- **Rule 01:** Bring pets in if you can. If you do not know where they are, do not go out.
- **Steps:**
  1. Pets in: state flip OUTSIDE to INSIDE.
  2. Keep them in 24 hours, wipe outside supplies: timer.
  3. Wash with shampoo or soap, gloves and mask: fill bar CLEAN.
  4. Several days of food and water in airtight containers: counter.
- **Clock framing:** FIRST 24 HOURS.
- **Sources:** CDC-IN: "Bring pets inside with you, if you can"; keep them indoors for at least 24 hours. UK-RAD: if you
  do not know where your pet is, do not go out to find it; "Once you are indoors, do not let your pets out." CDC-STAY:
  "Wash your pet carefully with shampoo or soap and water." READY-PETS: "Keep several days' supply of food in an
  airtight, waterproof container."

## 17. Is Food and Water Safe After Fallout?

- **Hook numbers:** boiling removes 0 radioactive material; sealed food is safe.
- **Fatal mistake:** boiling tap water and thinking it is now clean.
- **Rule 01:** Sealed and indoor food is safe. Bottled water first.
- **Steps:**
  1. Food already inside: tick SAFE.
  2. Sealed containers from outside: wipe, then SAFE (wipe sweep animation).
  3. Garden produce: stamp NOT UNTIL OFFICIALS SAY.
  4. Water: bottled first, then toilet tank and water heater: priority bars.
  5. Boiling does not remove radioactivity: state flip BOILED: STILL NOT CLEARED.
- **Clock framing:** FIRST 24 HOURS.
- **Sources:** CDC-STAY: "Boiling tap water will not remove radioactive materials."; "Foods and drinks that are
  sealed or that were already inside are safe." CDC-FAQ: "bottled water is the only water source that is certain to
  be free of contamination." Ready.gov flyer (topic 12 URL): "Do not eat fresh food from a garden."; toilet tank (not
  bowl) and water heater.

## 18. What the Siren Means (Sweden's Signal)

- **Hook numbers:** 7-SECOND bursts, 14-SECOND pauses; tested at 15:00 four times a year.
- **Fatal mistake:** ignoring it as "just a test" on a day that is not a test day.
- **Rule 01:** Go indoors, close windows, doors and ventilation, turn on the radio.
- **Steps:**
  1. Learn the pattern: waveform of 7 s on, 14 s off.
  2. Indoors now: door flips OPEN to SHUT.
  3. Ventilation off: fan state flip.
  4. Listen to Sveriges Radio P4: radio dial.
  5. All clear is one long 30-second tone: waveform.
- **Clock framing:** 2 MINUTES OF SIREN.
- **Sources:** SINGLE-COUNTRY topic. Krisinformation and SOS Alarm (from search excerpts; the krisinformation page
  returned 404 to our fetcher, re-check before scripting): https://www.sosalarm.se/en/112-and-other-important-numbers/important-public-announcements/
  and https://krisinformation.se/en/finding-help-and-services/emergency-warning ("Immediately go indoors, shut windows,
  doors, and ventilation. Listen to Sveriges Radio P4"; "seven-second sound bursts with a 14-second pause in between";
  tests on the first non-holiday Monday of March, June, September and December at 15:00; all clear "an uninterrupted
  30-second signal"). Lowest reach for a mostly English-speaking audience; consider a UK Emergency Alerts version.

   Figure 3 ("Credit: LLNL"). Treat as one source family; say "about" and show "SOURCE: LLNL / FEMA".
2. **The FEMA Third Edition planning guidance PDF** (over 10 MB) was not read directly; any line "FEMA says PF 40" or
   similar must not be used until someone reads its "Adequate Shelter" section (page 65 per the table of contents).
3. **Fallout arrival time** varies: "10 minutes or more" (Ready.gov), "at least 10 - 15 minutes" and "15 minutes or
   more" (FEMA PrepTalks), "several minutes" (LLNL). Use "10 minutes or more".
4. **EMP**: only REMM and FEMA-72 give consumer-relevant lines; no official numbers for cars or phones.
5. **EU 72-hour list** has no water quantity; use national figures and say whose.
6. **FCC, FEMA WEA, CDC carbon monoxide and Swedish warning pages** blocked or 404'd our fetcher; wording comes from
   official search excerpts or sister pages and needs a browser re-check.
7. **Swedish brochure (MCF, 2024)**: English PDF not found on mcf.se; no licence statement. Cite in words only.
