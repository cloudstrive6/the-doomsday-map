# 001 First 15 Minutes After a Nuclear Warning

## Hook

Your phone screams. Nuclear attack warning.

The first 15 minutes can save your life.

## Stakes

After a blast, you have 10 minutes or more before fallout arrives.

Then stay inside for at least 24 hours.

## The fatal mistake

Your instinct says: grab the car keys, outrun the fallout, fetch the kids. That's the fatal mistake.

A car is not a shelter. The roads will likely jam. Schools have their own shelter plans.

## The official rule

The official rule: get inside, stay inside, stay tuned. This is public guidance, not a forecast.

## Rule 1

Rule 1. Get inside the nearest sturdy building. Now.

Walls cut your dose. A car only halves it. A basement, about 10 times. The core of a big building, about 100 times.

## The 4 steps

Next, 4 steps, in this order.

## Step 1

1. Go down, or to the middle. Fallout settles on the roof and outer walls. Stay away from both.

## Step 2

2. Close windows and doors. Turn off fans and air con. Planners say a broken window won't ruin your shelter.

## Step 3

3. Stay inside for at least 24 hours, unless officials tell you otherwise.

## Step 4

4. Stay tuned on a battery or hand-crank radio. Fake alerts happen, so get inside first, then confirm on official radio, TV or alerts.

## Recap

Get inside. Go deep. Seal up. Stay 24 hours. Stay tuned. Save this. Send it to your family.

## Loop

Learn it now, before your phone screams.
